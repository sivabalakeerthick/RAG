About CogniDoc

Bridging the gap between vast organizational documents and AI.

Silo Elimination

Instead of employees searching through five different disconnected legacy platforms to find an internal policy, RAG provides a single natural-language interface across the organization's entire tech stack.

Model Agnostic

Your data pipeline and knowledge base live separately from the AI brain. If a faster, cheaper, or more accurate LLM launches tomorrow you can swap it in instantly without rebuilding your system.

Own Data Base

Every document and query is processed in your private enterprise environment, ensuring no data is shared with external AI providers.

Built for High-Security Enterprise Workflows

CogniDoc ensures your internal IP remains protected while empowering teams to access information instantly.

Strict Access Control & Role Scoping Role-based policies (RBAC) to ensure users only access screens they are authorized to see.
Zero Data Leakage Guarantee Your organizational documents and search queries are never used to train external public foundation models.
Instant Knowledge Updates As new policies or technical docs are uploaded, CogniDoc indexes chunks seamlessly for immediate query availability.
